Source: Financial Modeling Prep (filings, analyst estimates, Treasury yields and country risk premiums), pulled live through your FMP connector. Consensus covers revenue, SG&A, EBITDA, EBIT, net income and EPS only. Guidance is entered by hand from the company's latest release or call. H1/H2 sum the quarters; a half that mixes reported and consensus quarters is marked C. Costs are positive; cash outflows are negative. Multiples in the valuation block use today's price and enterprise value for every year.
The forecast runs P&L → cash flow → balance sheet. Cash is the balancing item, interest is earned and paid on opening balances, and D&A is calculated from capex. The DCF follows Aswath Damodaran's FCFF approach: CAPM cost of equity with a mature-market premium plus country risk, a synthetic-rating cost of debt, a five-year fade to stable growth, and terminal reinvestment of g ÷ ROIC. Stock-based compensation is treated as an operating expense.
