By sector

Sectors ordered by total estimated volume. Trades ranked largest first within each.

Members disclose each transaction as a dollar range, not an exact figure. Ranking uses the range midpoint; open-ended bands such as “Over $50,000,000” are estimated at 1.5× the floor and marked with a plus sign. Rows show the filed range alongside.

Sectors come from the FMP company profile of each ticker. Exchange-traded and mutual funds are grouped as “ETFs & Funds”; bonds, municipal securities and assets without a listed ticker are grouped as “Bonds, Munis & Other”. Options on a stock take that stock’s sector.

“Update from FMP” re-pulls the latest Senate and House disclosures through your Financial Modeling Prep connector in claude.ai, looks up sectors for any new tickers, and keeps the result in this browser. The committed report in the repository is regenerated with python -m investing.congress.
